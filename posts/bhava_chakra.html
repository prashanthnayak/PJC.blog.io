Bhavachakra Study Notes

Rāśi vs. Bhāva Systems

  • Rāśi-chakra — Nārāyaṇa tattva; immutable cosmic truth.
  • Bhāva-chakra — focuses on *self-perception* and possible delusion.
  • Bhāva-chalita — “I and the world” lens; the social creature within.

Dormant Houses

Houses lacking planets are like dormant experiences awaiting external triggers.

Travel & Mobility Indicators

  • General kāraka for travel: Venus.
  • Friday (Venus’ day) is especially favorable for journeys.
  • 3rd house — short trips.
  • 9th house — freedom to travel, visas, pilgrimages.
  • 12th house — foreign residence.
  • 4th ↔ 10th polarity: home (4th) vs. being away for duty (10th).

Kendras, Konas & Dushtānas

Kendras (1-4-7-10)

Pillars of life, directly accessible; created by Moon; a Viṣṇu principle.

Konas (1-5-9)

Houses of knowledge; created by Sun; a Lakṣmī principle.

Dushtānas (6-8-12)

Challenges & suffering; ruled by Śani concept.

Kona → Kendra shows awareness of the resources stored in its corresponding pillar.

A strong benefic in a Kendra steadily uplifts life.

5th & 9th Houses — Knowledge Spectrum

  • 5th — future, *Mantra-śāstra*; planning ahead.
  • 9th — accumulated past knowledge, blessings.
  • Rāhu/Ketu in 5th make future-visualization difficult.
  • Grahā-drishti resembles a book on the shelf—access knowledge when needed.

House Lords & Karma

Lord of a bhāva represents the karma tied to that sphere; its placement colors the story.

6th House (Brahmacharya)

  • 6th lord in 4th — marriage delay / moving from parents’ home.
  • 6th lord in 10th — “Conference Yoga”; may meet partner professionally.
  • 6th lord in 7th — spouse reluctant to stay.
  • 6th lord in Lagna — personal traits promote celibacy.
Planetary Flavors:
• Saturn — austerity, hygiene issues.
• Rāhu — doubts, cross-cultural factors.
Any lord occupying a Kendra—benefic or malefic—amplifies that house’s themes.

Prayer / Penance Axis

  • Placements in 5th & 9th reflect active prayer (5th) vs. penance (9th).
  • 6th lord in 8th — praying for celibacy; 8th indicates divorce themes.
  • 8th lord in 5th or 9th — may even pray for spouse’s death.

Navāṁśa Trines

Everything seen in Navāṁśa trines is inborn talent. Rāhu in a navāṁśa trine to Lagna can give exceptional future-planning ability.

Upachaya (Growth) Houses — 3, 6, 10, 11

Free-Will Arena

  • Lagna lord in 3rd or 6th — Dheemantha Yoga: sharp intellect.
  • Lagna lord in 10th or 11th — Siddha Yoga: fulfillment & accomplishments.

Functional Meanings

  • 3rd — chosen weapon / skill.
  • 6th — conquering vices & enemies.
  • 10th — work promises & societal output.
  • 11th — gains, networks.
